# Joint Active Passive RIS Assisted Deployment
## Overview

This project focuses on the deployment of a joint active and passive Reconfigurable Intelligent Surface (RIS) system. The goal is to enhance wireless communication performance by leveraging large scale deployment (N = 40-50) of both active and passive RIS elements to optimize signal propagation and coverage. 

## Methodology

The methodology involves the following key steps:
1. **System Design**: Define the architecture of the joint active-passive RIS system, including the placement of active and passive elements, and their respective roles in signal enhancement.
2. **Channel Modeling**: Develop accurate channel models that account for the effects of both active and passive RIS elements on signal propagation, including path loss, fading, and interference.
3. **Optimization Algorithms**: Implement AO (Alternating Optimization) algorithms to jointly optimize the configuration of active and passive RIS elements for improved signal quality and coverage.
4. **Simulation and Evaluation**: Conduct extensive simulations to evaluate the performance of the proposed system under various scenarios, including different user distributions, environmental conditions, and RIS configurations.

## Results
The results demonstrate significant improvements in signal strength, coverage, and overall system performance when utilizing the joint active-passive RIS deployment compared to traditional systems. Key findings include:
- Enhanced signal-to-noise ratio (SNR) and reduced bit error rates (BER) in various deployment scenarios.
- Improved coverage area and user connectivity, especially in challenging environments with obstacles and interference.
